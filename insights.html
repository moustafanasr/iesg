Accreditation as the Trust Layer for ESG Data

World Accreditation Day 2026

Data can be collected. Trust must be earned.

That is the challenge—and the opportunity—facing organizations as sustainability moves from ambition to accountability.

Our Founder & CEO, Dr. Ahmed Rushdy, joined leading experts at World Accreditation Day 2026 — hosted by the Egyptian Accreditation Council (EGAC) in partnership with the EU-funded TIGARA Project, implemented by UNIDO — for a panel on “Accreditation as the Trust Layer for ESG Data and Digital Product Passports,” discussing how accreditation strengthens confidence in ESG data, enabling businesses to meet the growing expectations of investors, regulators, and global markets.

As sustainability reporting enters a new era, success will not be defined by the number of commitments an organization makes, but by its ability to provide credible, transparent, and verifiable information.

At iESG Advisory, we believe that meaningful sustainability begins with trusted data and strong governance. By helping organizations build reliable ESG frameworks, we enable them to strengthen resilience, unlock sustainable finance opportunities, and compete with confidence in an increasingly transparent global economy.

We are proud to be part of this important conversation shaping the future of sustainability in Egypt and beyond.

Because sustainability creates value only when it is backed by trust.
