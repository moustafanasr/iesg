About CBAM

The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) is the EU’s mechanism for applying a carbon price to emissions embedded in imports of carbon-intensive goods. The interactive calculator and readiness questionnaire will remain a dedicated application/plugin in the production implementation.
