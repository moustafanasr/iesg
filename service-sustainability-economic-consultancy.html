Sustainability and Economic Consultancy

Strategic, Tailored, and Holistic Approaches
Talk to our experts

iESG Advisory provides comprehensive consultancy services for corporates and financial institutions, focusing on ESG strategy development, risk assessment, sustainable finance, policy impact advisory and economic analysis to support long-term sustainability and regulatory compliance.

For Corporates

  • ESG Strategy Development: In-depth analysis, roadmap creation, and alignment of ESG goals with business strategy.
  • Materiality Assessments: Data-backed identification of critical ESG factors and stakeholder priorities.
  • ESG Integration & Implementation Support: Policies, operational embedding and change management.
  • Stakeholder Engagement & Strategic Communication: Stakeholder and value-chain mapping with transparent engagement.

For Financial Institutions

  • ESG Risk Assessment for Investments: Due diligence and identification of ESG liabilities.
  • Portfolio Decarbonization Strategies: Customized frameworks that steer investments toward Net Zero pathways.
  • Sustainable Finance Product Development: Green bonds, impact investing and innovative financing mechanisms.

Navigating Regulations. Assessing Policy Impact.

  • Analyze the impact of existing and proposed economic policies and regulations on business operations.
  • Provide strategic advisory for navigating complex regulatory environments while optimizing business performance.
  • Develop policy engagement strategies that support positive regulatory outcomes.

Economic Analysis and Impact Assessment

  • Evaluate the economic sustainability and long-term viability of business practices.
  • Conduct impact assessments that measure the economic contribution of sustainability initiatives.
  • Develop strategies that enhance economic value creation while aligning with sustainability goals and stakeholder expectations.
