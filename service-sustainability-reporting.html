SRi-ESG

iESG Advisory offers comprehensive sustainability reporting services covering ESG reporting, climate-related disclosure and carbon footprint reporting. We support framework selection, data management, narrative development, report design, climate-risk analysis and measurable target setting.

ESG Reporting

  • Framework selection support for GRI, IFRS S1/S2, IIRC and other relevant standards.
  • Data systems optimization for collection, management and software selection.
  • Strategic narrative crafting that communicates achievements and improves stakeholder engagement.
  • Report design that improves readability and reinforces brand credibility.

Climate-Related Disclosure

  • Scenario-driven climate risk and opportunity analysis.
  • Climate governance and strategy integration aligned with TCFD-style recommendations.
  • Tailored metrics that turn climate data into practical management information.

Carbon Footprint Benchmarking and Target Setting

  • GHG emissions assessment using a life-cycle approach across activities, products and services.
  • Benchmarking and science-based target setting for emissions reduction.
  • Alignment with leading standards such as the Greenhouse Gas Protocol.
