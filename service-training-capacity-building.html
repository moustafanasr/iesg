iESG SERVICE

Training & Capacity Building

Empowering Your Sustainability Champions

Talk to our teamAll services

iESG Advisory offers comprehensive training programs that equip individuals and organizations with the knowledge and skills required to drive meaningful ESG progress. Programs combine current sustainability frameworks with practical, interactive learning tailored to specific needs.

  1. Sustainability Reporting: In-house workshops covering ESG reporting standards and pathways to recognized certifications.
  2. ESG for Decision-Makers: Executive-focused sessions on ESG, board responsibilities and long-term integration.
  3. Sector-Specific Training: Practical programs tailored to the environmental, social and governance challenges of specific industries.
  4. Customizable Delivery: Online courses, webinars and on-site workshops supported by modern LMS capabilities.
